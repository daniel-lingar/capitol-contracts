# WRH — Informational Description for Veteran-Support Review

**Status:** Development-stage information  
**Delivery authorization:** None

What Really Happened (WRH) is a non-clinical psychoeducational curriculum that teaches trauma responses and survival patterns as understandable mechanisms. It is not therapy, treatment, diagnosis, or crisis care, and it does not replace clinical services.

The developing materials use plain-language explanations, repetition, optional private reflection, and examples from Daniel Bret Lingar's memoir. Daniel is not a veteran. His lived-experience account is a teaching source, not a clinical case study or proof of program effectiveness.

The intended audience includes civilians and veterans, families, and recovery, reentry, peer-support, and community organizations. A C-PTSD diagnosis is not presented as an entry requirement.

The repositories preserve a master curriculum architecture and separate pilot-format drafts. Their presence does not establish an approved pilot, completed delivery, facilitator qualification, or permission to use the materials.

WRH has not been demonstrated to prevent suicide, reduce relapse or crisis utilization, or produce clinical improvements. This document makes no claim of VA approval, endorsement, funding, contracting, or readiness designation.

Any future organizational use requires the review and release conditions described in the [WRH Public Release Status](https://github.com/daniel-lingar/wrh-master-curriculum/blob/master/PUBLIC_RELEASE_STATUS.md). Federal-facing proposals also require appropriate procurement and legal review before submission.
