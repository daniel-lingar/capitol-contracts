# Capitol Contracts LLC

**Public company documentation hub for Capitol Contracts LLC.**

Capitol Contracts LLC develops structured, non-clinical psychoeducational materials, curriculum systems, implementation tools, and training frameworks for public education, peer-support settings, recovery environments, veteran-support programs, reentry settings, and institutional partners.

The company's flagship education system is **What Really Happened (WRH)** — a structured curriculum that teaches trauma responses, addiction patterns, freeze responses, shame cycles, nervous-system adaptation, and survival behavior as understandable mechanisms rather than character failures.

## Daniel Bret Lingar's Mission: From 17.5 to Zero

> **“I am building this to lower 17.5 to 0.” — Daniel Bret Lingar**

**17.5** refers to the average number of U.S. Veterans who died by suicide each day in **2023**. The Department of Veterans Affairs reported **6,398 Veteran suicide deaths that year** in its 2025 National Veteran Suicide Prevention Annual Report, released February 5, 2026.

The decimal is a daily average across a year—not half a person. Every death represents a whole person, a family, and a life that mattered. This is a dated statistic, not a live daily count.

**Zero is the goal: no Veteran lost to suicide.** That aspiration gives direction to Daniel's work on plain-language education, survival-pattern understanding, and reducing shame and misunderstanding.

This is a personal mission statement, not a claim of demonstrated effectiveness. WRH remains development-stage, non-clinical education with no current delivery authorization; it has not been demonstrated to prevent suicide or replace professional care.

### Why This Mission Matters Personally

Daniel Bret Lingar is not a Veteran. He lives with C-PTSD and describes knowing from his own experience how hard it can be to live with it. Encountering figures about C-PTSD among Veterans, alongside the 17.5 daily Veteran suicide figure, drew him toward this mission through a personal connection to the suffering behind the numbers.

This explains his motivation. It does not establish that C-PTSD caused those deaths, and 17.5 is not a count of Veterans with C-PTSD. The VA statistic describes Veteran suicide deaths overall.

**Source:** [U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, February 5, 2026](https://news.va.gov/press-room/va-releases-annual-veteran-suicide-prevention-report-updated-with-2023-data/).

## The Research Connection and the Current Figure

Research supports a connection between C-PTSD and suicidal thoughts and attempts in veterans. A 2023 study of 638 UK Armed Forces veterans in Northern Ireland found that probable C-PTSD was significantly associated with lifetime suicidal thoughts and attempts. It did not establish causation or explain the U.S. national death count.

**17.5 is the historical daily average for 2023 that drew Daniel to this mission.** The newer September 2026 VA report records **6,488 Veteran suicide deaths in 2024—an average of 17.7 per day**. Neither figure is a count of deaths attributed to C-PTSD.

## Who This Work Is For

The 17.5-to-zero statement expresses the Veteran suicide-prevention aspiration that drew Daniel toward this work. The intended audience is broader:

- **People living with C-PTSD**, whether or not they are Veterans.
- **People who use or have used substances to cope with distress**, including those who describe their use as self-medication, and people in addiction recovery.
- **Veterans living with trauma-related difficulties**, including those experiencing substance-use problems.
- **Rehabilitation facilities and recovery programs** seeking to understand trauma, coping patterns, shame, avoidance, and barriers to participation.
- **Families and loved ones of all these groups**, who need understandable language for what their loved one may be experiencing and how it affects relationships.

A central aim is to help people understand patterns that can be misunderstood, while retaining accountability for harmful behavior. Families and loved ones are an intended audience in their own right.

Self-medication describes a possible reason for substance use; it is not assumed to explain every person's addiction or establish a diagnosis. This audience statement describes the purpose of the work, not authorization to deliver WRH or evidence that it treats C-PTSD, addiction, or suicide risk.

## First-Time Visitor Summary

Capitol Contracts LLC is not presenting WRH as therapy, medical care, diagnosis, crisis care, or legal advice. WRH is a non-clinical education system designed to help organizations teach pattern recognition, reduce shame-based misreadings, and support safer peer/facilitator-led conversations.

**Primary audiences:** people living with C-PTSD; people who use substances to cope and people in recovery; Veterans; families and loved ones of all these groups; rehabilitation facilities and recovery organizations; veteran-support programs; reentry settings; peer-support networks; public education partners; and institutional reviewers.
